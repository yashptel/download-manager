Downloads, done properly.

OpenDM takes over downloads from your browser and makes them fast, resumable and organized. Save videos in the quality you want. Free and open source for Mac and Windows.

Fast and reliable

Every file over several connections at once

OpenDM splits each download into parts and fetches them in parallel, so large files finish sooner. Pause any time; downloads pick up where they left off, even after a restart.

  • Resumes anything. Network drops and reboots don't cost you the part you already have.
  • Retries on its own. A download that fails partway tries again by itself.
  • Fresh links. When a link expires, open the page again and OpenDM carries on with the new address.

You decide where it goes

Name it and file it before it starts

Each new download asks for its name and folder, and it is already under way while you decide. Files sort themselves into Video, Music, Documents, Programs and Compressed.

  • Start now or later. Queue downloads for the night with the scheduler.
  • Quiet when you want. Turn off the dialog and the progress window, each on its own.

OpenDM Connect

Video in the quality you want

The browser extension hands your downloads to OpenDM with the cookies they need, so files behind a sign-in work too. Hover a video to pick a resolution, or save just the audio.

  • Streams too. Finds HLS and DASH streams as well as plain video files.
  • Right-click to send. A link, an image, a selection, or every link on the page.
  • Alt-click to skip. Keep a single download in the browser.

And the rest

Everything a download manager should do

Speed limits

Cap all downloads together or each one, so browsing stays quick.

Scheduler

Start waiting downloads at night and pause them again in the morning.

Site logins

Saved per site in your keychain, asked for when a server wants one.

Proxies

Uses your browser's proxy, or one you set.

Menu bar and tray

Keeps running in the background, ready for the next download. Opens at login if you like.

Power-user settings

Connections, part size, retries, headers and any engine option you need.

Free, open source, no tracking

No account, no ads and no analytics. The extension talks only to the app on your own computer.
